WHO WE ARE

Opportunities for people. Progress for the planet.

Youth Environmental Opportunities (YEO) is an initiative designed to help young people and environmental professionals discover opportunities to learn, work, innovate, and contribute to a more sustainable world.

Our vision is to make environmental opportunities easier to discover and help more people take meaningful action for nature and communities.
