WHO WE ARE

Opportunities for people. Progress for the planet.

Established on 18 May 2016, Youth Environmental Opportunities (YEO) is a global youth-focused platform dedicated to connecting young people with opportunities that support environmental sustainability and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

YEO shares information about scholarships, internships, jobs, grants, fellowships, conferences, forums, campaigns, awards, competitions, and other opportunities that enable young people to learn, grow, collaborate, and make a positive impact.

We believe young people can drive meaningful change when they have access to the right information and opportunities. By making opportunity information more accessible, transparent, and widely available, YEO seeks to help young people overcome information barriers, develop their potential, and contribute to a more sustainable and equitable world.

Through global outreach and the sharing of relevant opportunities, YEO aims to inspire youth participation, encourage collaboration, and support the next generation of environmental leaders and changemakers.

🎯

Our Mission

To connect young people worldwide with accessible, relevant, and reliable information about opportunities that advance environmental sustainability and the Sustainable Development Goals, enabling them to develop their potential and contribute meaningfully to a better future.

🌍

Our Vision

A sustainable and pollution-free world where air, land, and water are clean, wildlife can thrive, and every young person has the opportunity to contribute to a healthier planet. YEO envisions a world where transparent access to information empowers young people to overcome barriers, pursue their aspirations, and contribute to environmental protection and sustainable development.

Opportunity Disclaimer

Youth Environmental Opportunities (YEO) shares information about opportunities offered by external organizations, institutions, and other providers. YEO is an independent information-sharing platform and is not necessarily affiliated with, endorsed by, or responsible for the organizations offering these opportunities.

Although YEO strives to share useful and reliable information, opportunity details—including eligibility criteria, deadlines, funding, application procedures, and availability—may change or contain inaccuracies. YEO does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or continued availability of any listed opportunity.

Users are strongly encouraged to visit the official website of the relevant opportunity provider and independently verify all information before applying. Applicants are responsible for ensuring they meet the requirements and follow the official application procedures.

YEO does not make application decisions on behalf of opportunity providers and cannot guarantee selection, funding, employment, admission, or participation. The final decision rests with the respective provider. YEO's role is to improve access to opportunity information and help young people discover pathways for learning, professional growth, environmental engagement, and sustainable development.
