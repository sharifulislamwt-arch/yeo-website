# YEO Live Opportunity Directory

Updated on 24 October 2026.

## Included
- `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`
- `assets/` — ten local WebP card images
- `SOURCES.md` — official source URLs and the deadline/eligibility summary

## Deploy
1. Keep `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` in the same web root.
2. Upload the complete `assets/` folder at that same level. Keep the filenames unchanged.
3. Upload `SOURCES.md` as the maintainer's verification record; it is not required for the page to run.
4. Deploy/refresh your hosting and test the search, category filters, each View Details modal, official links, and share buttons on the public URL.

## Important maintenance note
The ten listings were checked against the linked source pages on 24 October 2026. Provider information can change. The site calculates whether an item is past its displayed deadline, but it does not automatically fetch new opportunities from providers. Re-check the official pages and replace or remove expired listings regularly. The card images are representative artwork, not official photographs from the providers.

Facebook, X, and LinkedIn share actions use the deployed public YEO page URL with an opportunity-specific URL fragment. Test the share previews after deploying to your own domain.
